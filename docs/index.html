NO.1 WENDIGO

wendigo are creatures born out of hunger. tracing back to canadian folklore during winters when starved and left all alone in the snowy canadian mountaines the starving hunger would turn people into wendigos creatures with an insatiable hunger killing and eating all biological life in their way including humans.

No.2 YOTSUYA KAIDEN

In Japan there is an ages old play named YOTSUYA KAIDEN which started around 1825 it captures the story of lady Oiwa a hard working and devoted women married to a samurai named Tamiya Lemon. Lemon wanted to marry Oume the granddaughter of a wealthy neighbourhood doctor for status thus he and his friends devised a plan of poisoning Lady Oiwa leaving her with a disfigured face to give Lemon an excuse to marry Oume but the poison turned out to be extremely potent and killed Lady Oiwa. Her spirit did'nt move to the afterlife after her tragic death rather lingered transforming her into an onryo she took her revenge by driving lemon mad through hallucinations and he ended up accidently killing his new bride at his wedding night and later tricked him into killing his new in-laws through making him see her disfigured face on theirs turning him into a wanted man and the horrors didn't end there rather lemon kept getting more and more hallucinations and they ended only when Sato Yomoshichi Lady Oiwa's sister's husband tracked down Lemon and killed him putting an end to the bloody cycle of betrayal and revenge and bringing peace to Lady Oiwa's restless spirit even to this day YOTSUYA KAIDEN captures this tragic tale and actors,directors etc have to pay respect to Lady Oiwa's grave beforehand to avoid the curse,may her spirit rest in peace.

NO.3 THE LEGEND OF THE KITSUNE

The Kitsune is an ethereal, functionally immortal shape-shifter whose longevity stems from absorbing spiritual energy over centuries. These celestials possess reality-warping sorcery, manifesting kitsunebi (fox-fire) and illusions. They branch into thirteen distinct elemental types, most notably the benevolent, multi-tailed Zenko (celestial messengers of Inari) and the malevolent, chaotic Yako (field foxes).

NO.4 Gashadokuro

Part of the old yokai stories from japan the origins of this creature rise from war hunger and famine,it's said that when famines occured and a wars left unprotected the combined malice of the dead would end up forming the nocturnal specter towering over hundreds of meters tall the Gashadokuro is known to have an insatiable eternal hunger which would go on to make it eat humans.It would never target large groups of humans or any animals or plants but rather was a solitary hunter which only hunted and ate humans and after it's bloodthirst was temporarely satisfied or sun rise was near,it would collapse back into harmless mound of ordinary bones until the next night.The name Gashadokuro comes from the clattering sound of bones gachi-gachi that it would make while approaching it's targets where in kanji the specific letters used for gachi-gachi meant starvation or hunger,moreover the yokai had the ability to wander silently without making any noise as it arose after midnight and the prey would only hear the gachi-gachi noise by the time it was right beside them.The earliest accounts of the creature come from the legend of Takiyasha-hime the sorceress daughter of the samurai Taira no Masakado who led a rebellion against the central government and was killed in battle, Takiyasha-hime later took her revenge by using her occult arts summoned the giant nocturnal specter from the battlefield where bones of thousands of soliders lay unprotected.

No.5 Kuchisake-Onna

Mutilated by a jealeous samurai, a vain beauty transcended death to become japan's notorious urban legend.Cloacked in a trench coat and a deceptive surgical mask, this malevolent spirit stalks desolate alleyways. She corners unsuspecting travelers with a seemingly innocent question "Am i beautiful?".The trap snaps shut down when she unmasks,exposing a grotesque, ear-to-ear lacerated grin.A terryfying ultimatum follows: flatter her monstrous visage and have your own flesh severed to mirror hers,or reject her and face immediate, brutal decapitation by her rusted shears. Escaping the phantom demands swift psychological warfare , using ambigious riddles to paralyse her vanity long enough to flee.

No.6 Tsuchigumo (土蜘蛛) — The Earth Spider

Born from the vengeful remenants of the ancient, defeated clans who defied imperial rule,this titan morphed into an arachnid god. Seekers of hidden shrines stumbled upon it's lair,their awe instantly freezing into primal terror as the illusion dissolved into a skittering nightmare. Warriors repelled the beast using blessed steel, fire, and sacred prayers, shattering it's sorcery before being consumed by it's venemeous grasp.
